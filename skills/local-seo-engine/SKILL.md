# Local SEO Engine

Use this skill to improve local rankings in ways that actually move calls and revenue.

## Trigger on requests like

- audit this local business SEO
- improve GBP rankings
- what should we fix first for local SEO
- find category gaps
- build service plus city pages
- how many reviews do we need
- what backlinks should we target
- make a monthly local SEO report

## Goal

Focus on the highest-leverage local SEO actions, not vanity tasks.

## Core workflow

1. Fix Google Business Profile categories first.
2. Ignore broad vanity keywords unless they are already within reach.
3. Find bottom-funnel keywords with clear buyer intent.
4. Build one money page per service and city only when the page can be genuinely differentiated.
5. Increase review velocity with a realistic target and response templates.
6. Go after backlinks from real relevant sources, not garbage packages.
7. Track only the numbers tied to money.

## Output format

Return:
- **GBP category fixes**
- **Bottom-funnel keyword targets**
- **Money page plan**
- **Review velocity plan**
- **Backlink target list**
- **Monthly scorecard**
- **Next move**

## How to think

### 1. GBP categories
- Compare the business against top local competitors.
- Look for missing primary or secondary categories.
- Prioritize categories that unlock different local searches.

### 2. Keyword targeting
- Prefer terms like:
  - service + city
  - emergency + service
  - near me
  - high-intent problem phrases
- De-prioritize broad chest-beating keywords that do not convert.

### 3. Money pages
- One page per service per city can work.
- But cloned pages are trash.
- Only recommend this if pages can be meaningfully differentiated with real service details, proof, geography, FAQs, and intent alignment.

### 4. Reviews
- Reviews are not just trust. They are ranking fuel.
- Estimate the review gap against the top competitors.
- Turn that gap into a simple monthly target.
- Write response templates by star level, but keep them varied and natural.

### 5. Backlinks
- No directories, no packages, no link farms.
- Find real sites linking to multiple competitors but not to the client.
- Those are the first outreach targets.
- A few real links beat a pile of trash.

### 6. Reporting
Track only:
- rankings for money keywords
- calls or leads from GBP
- revenue tied to those leads when possible

## Rules

- Do not confuse SEO activity with SEO progress.
- Do not recommend thin city-page spam.
- Do not sell backlinks from junk sources.
- Do not hide behind traffic if calls are not improving.
- If the Google Business Profile is weak, start there before touching everything else.
