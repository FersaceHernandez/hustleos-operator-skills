# Local Business Site

This skill is for shipping a site that helps somebody get paid fast.

## Use cases

- new local business site
- one-page service offer
- same-day website delivery
- trust rebuild for cash businesses going legit
- simple portfolio/proof page for a service operator

## Core principles

- Simple beats fancy.
- One clear CTA beats multiple weak ones.
- Real proof beats generic copy.
- Static beats fragile.
- Ship today, improve tomorrow.

## Required inputs

Collect or infer:
- business name
- offer
- target customer
- service area
- CTA destination
- 1-3 proof points or credibility anchors

If some inputs are missing, make reasonable assumptions and move.

## Page structure

Default structure:
1. Hero with clear promise
2. Short credibility/proof band
3. Services or offer stack
4. How it works
5. Why choose us
6. FAQ
7. Final CTA

## Build rules

- Prefer static HTML/CSS/JS unless the user explicitly needs something heavier.
- Write copy at an 8th-grade reading level.
- Avoid agency-speak.
- Use a phone number, form, Stripe link, or booking link as the primary CTA.
- Make mobile the default design target.
- Keep sections tight. No dead weight.

## Deployment bias

Prefer hosts that do not depend on a personal laptop staying awake.

## Delivery checklist

Before calling it done:
- verify public load
- verify CTA link
- verify domain or staging URL
- capture at least one proof screenshot
- hand back the live link, not just files
