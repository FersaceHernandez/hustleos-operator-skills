# GEO Research: Princeton Study

**Paper:** GEO: Generative Engine Optimization
**Authors:** Princeton University, IIT Delhi, Georgia Tech, Allen Institute for AI
**Published:** November 2023 (arXiv:2311.09735) | Accepted: KDD 2024
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735

## The 9 Methods

| Method | Boost | How |
|--------|-------|-----|
| Cite Sources | +40% | Add authoritative citations and references |
| Statistics | +37% | Include specific numbers and data points |
| Quotations | +30% | Add expert quotes with attribution |
| Authoritative Tone | +25% | Use confident, expert language |
| Easy-to-Understand | +20% | Simplify complex concepts |
| Technical Terms | +18% | Include domain-specific terminology |
| Unique Words | +15% | Increase vocabulary diversity |
| Fluency | +15-30% | Improve readability and flow |
| Keyword Stuffing | **-10%** | AVOID — hurts visibility |

## Best Combinations

| Combo | Use Case |
|-------|----------|
| Fluency + Statistics | Highest overall boost |
| Citations + Authoritative Tone | Professional content |
| Easy Language + Statistics | Consumer content |
| Technical Terms + Citations | Academic/scientific |

## Key Findings

- Maximum visibility boost: up to 40%
- Low-ranking sites can gain up to 115% with citations
- Validated on Perplexity.ai (commercial AI search)
- AI search engines cite sources — they don't rank pages
