## Appendix: The 9 Princeton GEO Methods

Based on research from Princeton University on how to optimize content for AI search engines:

1. **Cite Sources (+40%)** — Reference authoritative sources. AI engines prioritize content that cites credible data.
2. **Statistics (+37%)** — Include specific numbers. "Revenue grew 340%" beats "revenue grew significantly."
3. **Expert Quotes (+30%)** — Add quotes with named attribution. Builds trust signals.
4. **Authoritative Tone (+25%)** — Write confidently. No hedging language.
5. **Easy-to-Understand (+20%)** — Simple explanations of complex topics. Short paragraphs.
6. **Technical Terms (+18%)** — Use domain-specific vocabulary correctly.
7. **Unique Words (+15%)** — Diverse vocabulary signals expertise.
8. **Fluency (+15-30%)** — Natural, well-flowing prose that reads well aloud.
9. **Keyword Stuffing (-10%)** — AVOID. Hurts AI visibility. Natural language only.
